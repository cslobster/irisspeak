Training

Training IrisSpeak-135M

IrisSpeak-135M is SmolLM2-135M-Instruct fine-tuned to predict the next card. IrisSpeak-135M was trained on all 69,676 states with a 6-card cap, end-of-message down-weighting, a topic-bias filter, and question-answer states weighted up to 37 percent of each epoch. On an Apple M2 Max, two weighted epochs, 4,356 steps, batch 32, 125 minutes.

How each evaluation works

  • Training loss is the cross-entropy between the model's distribution over 3,096 outputs and the soft target set for the state, which puts weight on every acceptable next card, not just one.
  • Dev Recall@k is measured every 500 steps on 1,000 held-out states: the share of states where at least one acceptable next card is in the model's top k. Recall@16 is the grid; Recall@100 is the pool the reranker sees.
  • End-of-message precision is how often "end" is right when it is the top prediction. It is the guard against a model that ends utterances too early.
  • Test sets at the end: the general held-out set and the question-answer subset where the partner asked a question and an AAC user replied in at most 5 cards. See the benchmarks page.

card representation3,096 trainable rows (cards, name slot, end) initialised from the mean embedding of each spoken form; fed as input embeddings; the same rows form the output head
promptsetting, last two turns, partner utterance as text, then a start marker and the tapped cards
losssoft-target cross-entropy at the last position, over card rows only
optimiserAdamW, 3e-5 backbone, 1e-3 card rows, warm-up 200 steps, linear decay, grad clip 1.0
batch, epochs32 states; two weighted epochs of 69,676 states
hardwareApple M2 Max, MPS backend, fp32, about 0.6 s per step
