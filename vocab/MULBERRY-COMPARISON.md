# Mulberry Symbols versus the IrisSpeak vocabulary

Source: mulberrysymbols.org, release 3.6.1 (3436 SVG symbols, 3163 distinct names; licence CC BY-SA 2.0 UK). Vocabulary: `vocab/vocab.csv`, 3094 cards.

Matching: symbol file names normalised to words ("go_,_to" → go; "happy_lady", "dad_parent", "relax_2" → happy, dad, relax) and compared with card labels, spoken forms and aliases, allowing singular/plural and "to <verb>" forms.

## Recommendation

- Use the full Mulberry set for pictures now: it raises picture coverage from 688 to 1,216 cards (528 emoji cards gain a symbol; 43 current pictures come from other sets), no vocabulary change. Done on 2026-09-08.
- The corpus-backed single words above were added on 2026-09-09 as search-only cards (`vocab/extra_cards.csv`): they appear in View all with their Mulberry picture and can be spoken, but the model still has 3,096 rows and will not suggest them until the next retrain. Names of people stay in the name slot, not as cards.
- Do not import the rest of Mulberry: about 1927 extra nouns (flags, maps, professions, kitchenware) would dilute the model's 3,094-way choice without corpus support.
- 1921 cards have no Mulberry picture, mostly function words, phrases, feelings and describing words. Keep the emoji fallback there, or add ARASAAC for the feelings and social phrases.
